# Research summary

The initial research compared four Android falling-block projects. Historical versions and source links are recorded in [the upstream inventory](../upstream-inventory.md).

GBLOCKS uses its own Kotlin game core and Compose renderer. The research favored separate rules, input, rendering, and audio responsibilities rather than importing an upstream application architecture.

## Historical license findings

- Simplis: no license found; source reuse was excluded.
- TetrisLite: Apache-2.0; reuse would require applicable license and notice obligations.
- compose-tetris: MIT; reuse would require applicable copyright and license notices.
- Fluidtris: no license found; source reuse was excluded.

These findings describe the September 24, 2026 research snapshot, not a fresh audit. Repository licenses do not grant rights to unrelated branding, artwork, or recordings.
